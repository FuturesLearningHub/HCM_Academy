HCM Academy · Assignment Changes

Move a manager's direct reports.

A manager is leaving, moving, or handing off their team. Move the team to a new manager in one transaction, from the manager's record.

Oracle HCM · Direct Reports
Step 1 of 9 · Before you start

One person, or the whole team?

  1. One person needs a new manager: use Change Manager on that person.
  2. Several people who report to the same manager: use Direct Reports on the manager. That is this video.
Decide first
Watch-outs

Three things to remember.

  1. Do this before you terminate the manager. Reports left on a terminated manager leave approvals with no one to act on them.
  2. If the manager is leaving, date the move on or before their last day.
  3. Oracle moves at most 50 reports at a time. Split larger teams into batches.
