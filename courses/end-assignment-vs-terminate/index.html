1 · Intro

When to use it.

Oracle has two ways to end work. End Assignment stops one job. Terminate Employment ends the whole work relationship and every assignment in it, including jobs in other areas.

Picking the wrong one either leaves a job running that should have stopped, or stops a job the person is keeping. When someone holds more than one job, check every job and talk to the other HCM before you submit.

Use this whenSomeone is leaving a job, and you need to decide whether they leave one job or Vanderbilt employment, including when they hold more than one job.

Before you submit

The same four checks on every pay-impacting transaction. Draft: the owner for this area confirms anything marked To confirm.

Attach

None named yet.

To confirmNo document is named yet, and who submits when the jobs sit in different business units is an open item.

Check

Read every field back against the source document: person, effective date, position, hours, pay, and costing.

  • Every active assignment is listed and the primary one is found.
  • Leaving all jobs is Terminate Employment. Leaving only yours is End Assignment.
  • The HCM who owns the other assignment knows.
  • Primary is changed first if your job is primary and they keep another.

Cutoff

Submit before the HCM Transaction Cutoff so pay stops on time. For monthly pay it is the 18th in most months; check the HR/Payroll Calendar.

Who does what

  • Who starts it: You, with the other assignment's HCM.
  • Who approves: To confirm: the approval chain for this transaction.
  • What happens next: Terminate Employment stops pay for every job. End Assignment stops only yours.

Watch-outs

  • Terminating someone who keeps another job stops pay for that job too. Check assignments before you terminate.
  • Open item: confirm which End Assignment entry point Vanderbilt uses (Change Assignment > End Assignment or the termination page), since it depends on the termination version configured.
  • Terminate Employment on a person who keeps another job stops pay for that job too.
  • Move any direct reports on the ending assignment to a new manager before it ends.
  • Open item: confirm Vanderbilt's rule for who submits when the jobs sit in different business units.

HCM Academy job aid · Offboarding

End an assignment or terminate employment, including multiple assignments

Use when: Someone is leaving a job, and you need to decide whether they leave one job or Vanderbilt employment, including when they hold more than one job. · System: Oracle HCM

  1. Count the person's assignments in Employment Info.
  2. Leaving everything? Use Terminate Employment.
  3. Keeping at least one job? End only the job that stops.
  4. Ending the only assignment? You must terminate.
  5. Ending the primary assignment? Make another one primary first.
  6. List every active assignment and find the primary one.
  7. Ask: is the person leaving all jobs or only yours?
    • Keeps a job: End Assignment on yours
    • Leaves all: Terminate Employment
  8. Coordinate with the HCM who owns the other assignment.
  9. If your job is primary and they keep another, change primary first.
  10. Submit End Assignment or Terminate Employment with the right dates.
  11. Confirm the result in Employment Info.
