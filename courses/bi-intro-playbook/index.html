1 · Intro

When to use it.

Oracle Transactional Business Intelligence (OTBI) reports on live Oracle HCM data. You use it to check your area's people, assignments, and costing without opening records one at a time. The Departmental Playbook gives HCMs a starting set of reports.

Most of your reporting is running a report someone already built and reading the results correctly. The key choices are the prompts you answer and the date you run it for.

Use this whenYou need a list or check across your area, such as everyone's costing, or you need to run an existing report and trust what it shows.

Watch-outs

  • Bad data in, bad data out. A report only reflects what was entered in Oracle.
  • Do not save changes over a shared report. Save your own copy in My Folders.
  • Open item: confirm where the Departmental Playbook lives in the catalog (folder path) and whether Vanderbilt exposes Reports and Analytics on a different menu.
  • Date matters. A person hired next week will not appear on a report run as of today.
  • Results show only people within your data security.

Reporting basics: BI reporting, the Departmental Playbook, and running reports

Use when: You need a list or check across your area, such as everyone's costing, or you need to run an existing report and trust what it shows. · System: Oracle BI (OTBI)

  1. Open Reports and Analytics (Navigator > Tools > Reports and Analytics).
  2. Know the folders: Shared Folders for official reports, My Folders for yours.
  3. Search or browse to the report, then click its name to open it.
  4. Use the Departmental Playbook costing reports.
    • HCM Assignment Cost Allocation Plan
    • HCM Element Cost Allocation Plan
    • HCM Cost Allocation Verification Plan
  5. Expect to see only the people your security allows.
  6. Open Reports and Analytics and find the report.
  7. Answer the prompts.
  8. Set the as-of or effective date.
  9. Run the report and wait for results.
  10. Read the results with the prompts in mind.
  11. Rerun with new prompts instead of editing the report.

Watch-outs

  • Bad data in, bad data out. A report only reflects what was entered in Oracle.
  • Do not save changes over a shared report. Save your own copy in My Folders.
  • Open item: confirm where the Departmental Playbook lives in the catalog (folder path) and whether Vanderbilt exposes Reports and Analytics on a different menu.
  • Date matters. A person hired next week will not appear on a report run as of today.
  • Results show only people within your data security.
  • Open item: confirm which standard Oracle HCM reports Vanderbilt expects HCMs to run and where they are stored.
