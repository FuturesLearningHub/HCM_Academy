1 · Intro

When to use it.

Job architecture is a basic HR framework: it groups an organization's jobs by the kind of work and the level of the work, so similar jobs are titled, leveled, and paid consistently.

Learn the terms first. Part 2 shows how Vanderbilt's job architecture is set up in Oracle.

Use this whenYou are new to job architecture, or you need to read a job title, family, or level before you build a position or requisition.
